## Defensible submission claim

VeerIQ repairs approved snapshot-derived fields through a shared permission dispatcher while preserving current deletions, revocations, holds, later edits, and tenant boundaries. Orders and declaratively imported datasets reuse the same runtime; a recorded scripted import uses live ArmorIQ and produces signed evidence that verifies offline against the published operator key. Across six recorded live batches (84 trials on different code versions), the model acted in 24 trials and attempted the poisoned action in 23; ArmorIQ plan binding blocked all 23, with zero forbidden effects, and the permitted repair completed in 18 of the 24. A six-trial pre-fix diagnostic completed five repairs; its partial-manifest failure led to a tested planning preflight. No batch finished its 40 requested trials, and 60 trials never acted, so the completion target is not established. The post-fix cohort has one of 40 trials recorded and cannot be resumed after the 14 October changes. A scripted live ArmorIQ repair also exports a plan hash and five service-returned audit IDs under an offline-verifiable operator signature. Sponsor HOLD and production deployment remain unobserved.
