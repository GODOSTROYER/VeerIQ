# VeerIQ recovery workspace — direction contract

## Scope and visitor mode
Operate. A judge or analyst chooses a synthetic recovery scenario, reviews its exact scope, follows the enforcement decisions, and inspects what changed and what stayed protected. The central task is repairing authorized damaged fields without erasing valid decisions made later.

## Audience, job and action
The primary action is to review a recovery scenario’s proposed grant and launch its run. Approval applies to an exact, bounded repair. A visitor can inspect the live run, scripted re-enactments, approvals, architecture, and recorded proof from the shared application shell.

## Chosen direction
The merged product is a durable recovery workbench with VeerIQ’s own shield and wordmark. Preserve the established charcoal/orange identity, Sunflower display face, Geist interface type, Geist Mono evidence labels, and two-pixel corners. The interface is flat and operational: fine borders and surface tones group connected evidence. The ArmorIQ Flow State reference informed the palette and type pairing; it does not imply shared ownership or endorsement.

## First viewport
The sticky shell shows VeerIQ identity, current system mode, theme control, local demo identity, and navigation. Mission Control introduces the recovery task, places incident context and preserved decisions near the proposed action, and shows scenarios and system evidence in responsive sections. At narrow widths, navigation and evidence stack while labels and actions remain readable.

## Signature interaction
The run view traces each proposed action through runtime, ArmorIQ, VeerIQ grant scope, domain rules, second-person approval, and PostgreSQL. The recorded decision and evidence identify which layer stopped or allowed a request. A committed repair updates durable PostgreSQL state; later deletions, revocations, and edits remain protected. Run details and export expose the exact repair, event history, and receipt.

## Proof and constraints
PostgreSQL is the durable system of record in both live and scripted flows. Live ArmorIQ policy results, scripted planner behavior, offline simulation, and local receipt signing are explicitly distinguished in status and run labels. Synthetic incident data and local demo identities are clearly identified. Locally signed Ed25519 evidence must be described as local proof, not a remote ArmorIQ signature. One approval authorizes only the exact reviewed repair; the database re-checks and commits it transactionally. No claim of endorsement, certification, or universal injection resistance.

## Responsive and accessibility contract
Support the implemented light and dark themes across the shell and pages. Preserve visible keyboard focus, semantic outcome labels, and reduced-motion behavior. Narrow layouts stack content without losing complete field names, status text, or evidence access.

## Source of truth
Visual tokens live in `frontend/src/styles/tokens.css`; global type, focus, reduced-motion, two-pixel corner, and recovery-boundary rules live in `frontend/src/styles/global.css`. Bundled font faces are imported in `frontend/src/main.tsx`. The merged reference captures are under `.impeccable/review/merged-*`.
